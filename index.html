SwipeUnlock

Privacy Policy · Last updated: May 2026

1. Overview

SwipeUnlock is an independent accessibility app designed to help users with physical disabilities unlock their Android device hands-free.

2. Data Collection

SwipeUnlock does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. No user information is shared with third parties — ever.

3. Accessibility Service

SwipeUnlock uses Android's Accessibility Service solely to perform automated swipe gestures and unlock patterns on the lock screen. The service does not read screen content, monitor keystrokes, or observe any user activity beyond what is necessary to execute the unlock gesture.

4. Local Storage

Settings such as button size, transparency, and pattern positions are stored locally on your device only (Android SharedPreferences). This data never leaves your device.

5. No Ads, No Tracking

SwipeUnlock contains no advertisements, no tracking, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs of any kind.

6. No Internet Connection

The app requires no internet connection and makes no connections to external servers.

7. Children

SwipeUnlock is not directed at children under the age of 13.

8. Changes

Any changes to this privacy policy will be communicated through app updates on the Play Store.

9. Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, please contact the developer via the Play Store listing.
